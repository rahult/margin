# Margin — product brief

Margin is an active-reading environment for documents people cannot afford to skim: RFCs, ADRs, specifications, policies, and business cases.

## Hypothesis

A long document becomes less intimidating when it is presented as an argument to navigate rather than pages to consume. A reader is more likely to finish and retain a document when the product supplies:

1. A visual reading map.
2. A question before each section.
3. Synchronized text-to-speech.
4. Notes tied to the reader's interpretation.
5. A comprehension check at the end.

## Prototype loop

**Orient → Read/listen → Answer a question → Capture a thought → See the argument evolve → Prove comprehension.**

## Deliberate exclusions

- No generative AI backend yet.
- No PDF or Google Docs ingestion yet.
- No accounts or cloud sync.
- No points, badges, or streaks detached from comprehension.

## Commercial direction

Open-source the local reader and document engine. Monetize team workspaces, enterprise connectors, governed AI, organisational knowledge graphs, analytics, and private deployment.
